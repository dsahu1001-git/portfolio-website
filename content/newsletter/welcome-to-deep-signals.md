Deep Signals is a reviewed weekly briefing on meaningful developments in artificial intelligence and quantum computing.

Each edition will focus on a small number of developments worth understanding:

- important research and product announcements
- practical implications for engineering leaders and builders
- links to original sources
- a clear distinction between evidence, analysis, and speculation

The goal is not to summarize every headline. It is to make the signal easier to find.
